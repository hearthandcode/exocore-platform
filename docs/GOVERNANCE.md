# Governance posture

Exocore Platform starts from a simple stance: assistance should make it easier to return to meaningful work without quietly taking ownership of that work.

## Current principles

- **Propose before changing.** A useful suggestion should retain enough context for someone to inspect its purpose, scope, and source.
- **Keep authority legible.** A view, index, or generated summary should not silently mutate the records it displays.
- **Make change reversible.** A durable change needs a human-visible route back, along with evidence that reversal preserves integrity.
- **Treat consent as specific and revocable.** Retaining an operational event is not the same as permission to infer patterns from it or inspect the contents of a person's artifacts.
- **Keep early scope small.** The orientation shell has no record authority, local-file access, network services, provider accounts, analytics, or background automation.

## What this does not mean

It does not mean a person must approve every small interaction. It means that the system must not conceal a meaningful boundary crossing behind convenience. The question is not whether assistance is allowed. The question is whether the person can understand, refuse, and reverse the consequences.

## Invitation

If this stance resonates, useful feedback names a concrete moment where a tool made returning to work easier or harder, and explains what authority or context was missing.
